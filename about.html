ABOUT

Raised in Las Vegas, siopaolo is a singer-songwriter who evokes his listeners' emotions from heart-wrenching ballads, to more light-hearted melodies. siopaolo's music will make sure to find its way to your heart one way or another.

Since siopaolo's 2023 release of his hit single, "better for you", the Filipino-American artist has reached the hearts across the world with new singles and his 4 song debut EP, "in my dreams." From affectious R&B to sweet and cute jazz, siopaolo's ever evolving discography kindle the listeners' spirits.

Now, siopaolo is currently working on turning his dreams of performing his music on stage to reality.

GENRES Indie Pop, Bedroom Pop, R&B, Pop
HOMETOWN Las Vegas, Nevada
